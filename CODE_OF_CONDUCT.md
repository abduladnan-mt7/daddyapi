# Code of Conduct

daddyapi follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful, especially with people making their first contribution.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language, or publishing others' private information.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact the maintainer, [@abduladnan-mt7](https://github.com/abduladnan-mt7), privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban a contributor, for behavior that breaks this code, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
